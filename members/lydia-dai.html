About Lydia

Lydia is a final-year master’s student in Environmental Sciences and Policy Analysis, interested in nature-based climate solutions and their potential to unlock finance for adaptation and resilience in the Global South. Her research adopts a policy and finance lens to examine how country-level risks shape funding and lending decisions for ecosystem-based adaptation, with the aim of supporting more enabling policy environments and improved decision-making processes.
